About

Chris Anderson is a software engineer with more than 20 years of experience building and supporting systems across both Federal Aviation Administration and Department of Defense.

This site serves as a place to share projects, technical experiments, thoughts on software engineering, and lessons learned.

Chris has always been drawn to technology not only for the engineering itself, but for the ability to continuously learn, adapt, and build useful things.

Outside of technology, he spends most of his free time trying to improve his golf game and staying active.

At the end of the day, he's just having fun trying to figure things out and share his knowledge.
